Our story

From picture palace to market hall

Before the stalls came the screen: the site had an earlier life as a local cinema, and the building still carries the shape of it — one long looping corridor where the audience used to be.

Since 1955 it’s been a market: thirty-odd small shops trading side by side while Wood Street changed around them. The faces turn over; the horseshoe holds.

Getting here

Wood Street station is 2 minutes on foot — 17 minutes from Liverpool Street on the Overground. The W16 bus stops right outside; the 123, 212, 230, 275 and W12 are a short walk.
