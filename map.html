The Market Map

One corridor, bent into a U, with shops down both sides of the walkway. Market Side units line the outer wall and Antique City units the inner block, and both run the same way round: in from Wood Street, up one arm, across the top and back down the other. The map shows the order the units come in rather than measured distances, so it is a visitor guide, not an architectural plan. Tap any unit to open that shop.
