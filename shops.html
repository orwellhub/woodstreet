The Shops

25 independents across 34 units, Tuesday to Saturday. Every shop is run by the person behind the counter, so most keep their own days and hours inside the market’s opening times. Ring ahead if you are making the trip for one in particular.

9 units are currently available to let: 4 in Antique City (A4, A7, A11, A17/18) and 5 in Market Side (4, 5, 31, 33, 38). Enquire about taking one on.
