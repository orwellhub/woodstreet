Getting here

By trainWood Street station is a 2-minute walk — turn right out of the station and the market is on your left. Around 17 minutes from Liverpool Street on the Overground; 7 from Chingford. Walthamstow Central (Victoria line) is roughly a 15-minute walk.
By busThe W16 stops right outside. The 123, 212, 230, 275 and W12 stop within a short walk.
By bike or carCycle racks near the station (to be confirmed). Street parking is limited and mostly pay-and-display — the train really is easier.

Getting around inside

The market trades on one level inside, around a single looping corridor. Some units are snug and browsing can be close-quarters — traders are quick to help reach or fetch things.

Entrance thresholds, accessible facilities and baby-changing details are being confirmed for this page. In the meantime, ring 020 8521 0410 and we’ll talk your visit through.

Well-behaved dogs on leads are welcome in the corridor; food units ask them to wait outside.

Know before you go

One corridor, thirty doors, no wrong turns. If you’re hunting something specific, check the map or the directory before you set out.
